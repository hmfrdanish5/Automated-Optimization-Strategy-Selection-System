Limitations:
    The dataset is synthetic. The TAC language is simplified. Optimization semantics use project-specific assumptions. Weighted operation costs are relative educational weights. The feature representation is intentionally small, so data-flow context is not fully represented and some identical feature vectors have different labels. These results are not production compiler performance. Grouped cross-validation does not remove the limits of synthetic data. Classification accuracy, optimization gain, and confidence are separate measurements.
